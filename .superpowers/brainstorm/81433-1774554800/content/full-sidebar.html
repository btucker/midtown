Design decisions reflected:

Needs Attention (top)
4 types: completed tasks ✓, threads waiting ↩, @mentions @, stale work ⏱. Each dismissible.
Tasks (unified)
Flat list, progress bars, channel tags. Completed today collapsible. No status dots.
Channels (clean)
No task pips. Threads inline at same font size. Closable ✕. Drag to reorder. Unread dots.
Thread lifecycle
Active → shows under channel. Needs you → moves to Attention. Idle → auto-closes. Manual close ✕.
